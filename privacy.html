Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Samurai Dash handles information when you play the game.

Samurai Dash is developed and published by sho_gd, an individual trader, and is distributed through Steam and the Google Play Store.

1. Information we collect

The game does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer. It does not ask you to create an account or provide personal information to play.

2. Ads, analytics and tracking

The game contains no advertisements, advertising trackers, analytics tools, or gameplay telemetry. We do not track players for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

3. Local save data

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device to support gameplay. The game does not send this information to the developer. Any cloud-save or synchronisation features provided independently by a platform are subject to that platform's own practices.

4. Third-party platforms

Steam and Google Play may independently process information for purchases, downloads, account management, security, and platform features. This processing is controlled by the relevant platform, not by the game itself.

5. Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The distribution platforms may apply their own age-related requirements and privacy practices.

6. Data retention and your rights

Because the developer does not collect personal data through the game, we do not maintain a database of player personal information. Information independently processed by a platform is subject to that platform's policies and applicable law.

7. Changes to this policy

If the game's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated. The latest version will be published at this URL with a revised update date.
